From idea to income in five steps

  1. STEP 1

    Design

    Describe the part in plain English, or upload an STL or STEP file. Every dimension stays editable.

  2. STEP 2

    Check

    Automatic checks for wall thickness, hole sizes and machine limits, with one-click fixes.

  3. STEP 3

    Protect

    The geometry is fingerprinted, timestamped and compared with every design on DesignShip.

  4. STEP 4

    Make

    Instant quotes across 3D printing, CNC and molding. A nearby partner shop builds and ships it.

  5. STEP 5

    Earn

    List it in one click. You earn a royalty on every order, plus a share of every remix.
